## What to Say for Common Supplier Questions

### "How long have you been in business?"
"NICE was founded in 1986 and has been a leader in contact center technology for over 35 years. CXone has been in market since 2016; Cognigy since 2017."

### "Are you financially stable?"
"NICE is publicly traded on NASDAQ (NICE) with ~$2.7B in annual revenue (FY2024) and a strong balance sheet. Audited financials are available at investors.nice.com."

### "Do you have references in our industry?"
"Yes. NICE serves 25,000+ customers globally across financial services, retail, healthcare, telecom, and government. We can provide references specific to your industry and company size under NDA."

### "What is your data breach notification process?"
"In the event of a confirmed breach affecting customer data, NICE notifies affected customers within 72 hours (aligned with GDPR Article 33 requirements), provides a detailed incident report within 30 days, and cooperates with regulatory notification requirements. Full incident response procedures are documented in the MSA and available for review."

### "Do you have a disaster recovery plan?"
"Yes. CXone maintains a formally documented Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery plan, tested annually. RTO < 4 hours, RPO < 1 hour. Test results available under NDA."
